## 1. Logging In to the Dashboard

1. Navigate to `/wp-admin/` in your browser.
2. Enter your **Username / Email** (`admin`) and **Password** (`password`).
3. Click **Log In**.

---

## 2. Managing Vanilla Products

The custom **Vanilla Products** module allows you to showcase vanilla bean grades with detailed laboratory test numbers for international B2B buyers.

### Adding a New Vanilla Product:
1. In the left sidebar, click **Vanilla Products** &rarr; **Add New Product**.
2. **Title**: Enter the full commercial product name (e.g. *Indonesian Planifolia Gourmet Vanilla Beans (Grade A)*).
3. **Description (Content)**: Describe the bean's flavor profile, aroma, oiliness, and recommended food/industrial applications.
4. **Featured Image**: On the right sidebar, click **Set featured image** and upload a high-quality photo of the pods.
5. **Vanilla B2B Export Specifications Box**:
   - **Grade / Classification**: e.g., `Gourmet Grade A` / `Extraction Grade B`
   - **Vanillin Content (%)**: e.g., `2.0% - 2.4%`
   - **Moisture Content (%)**: e.g., `30% - 35%`
   - **Pod Length (cm)**: e.g., `16 - 20 cm`
   - **Origin / Region**: e.g., `Bali & Papua, Indonesia`
6. Click **Publish**.

## 3. Managing Harvest & Curing Gallery

1. In the left sidebar, click **Gallery** &rarr; **Add New Photo**.
2. **Title**: Step or activity name (e.g. *Sun Drying on Elevated Decks*).
3. **Featured Image**: Upload photo from the farm or curing facility.
4. **Description**: Add 1–2 sentences explaining the quality step.
5. Click **Publish**.

---

## 4. Publishing SEO Articles

1. In the left sidebar, click **Posts** &rarr; **Add New Post**.
2. Write article title, body, add categories (e.g. *Market Trends*, *Harvest Updates*), and set a featured image.
3. Click **Publish** — the post will automatically appear on `/articles/`.

---

## 5. Editing Static Pages

- Go to **Pages** &rarr; **All Pages**.
- Click **Edit** on any page (*Home*, *About Us*, *Contact Us*, *Products*, *Gallery*).

---

## 6. Managing Navigation Menus

1. Go to **Appearance** &rarr; **Menus**.
2. Ensure **Primary Navigation Menu** is selected.
3. Rearrange or add new custom links as needed.
4. Click **Save Menu**.

---

## 7. Media Library Best Practices

- **Image Dimensions**: Recommended 1200x800px for products and gallery items.
- **File Format**: WebP or compressed JPG (under 300 KB) for maximum page speed.
- **Alt Text**: Always add descriptive English alt text (e.g. `indonesian-planifolia-vanilla-beans-grade-a`) for international SEO ranking.
